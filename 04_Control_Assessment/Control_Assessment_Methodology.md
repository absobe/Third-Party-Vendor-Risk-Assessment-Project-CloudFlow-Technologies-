# Control Assessment Methodology

**This is a fictional cybersecurity case study created for educational and portfolio purposes.**

## Approach

Each major control is rated using only the simulated evidence pack and questionnaire. Claims without artifacts are not rated Effective.

## Result scale

Effective · Partially Effective · Ineffective · Not Applicable · Evidence Not Provided

## Examples used in this case study

**CTRL-AC-001** — MFA for privileged users. Evidence: Access Control Policy. Result: **Effective**. Production CRM operator access and AWS privileged roles require MFA.

**CTRL-TPRM-004** — Formal subprocessor risk assessment. Evidence: Subprocessor Management Summary. Result: **Partially Effective**. Subprocessors are reviewed, but there is no fully standardized formal risk scoring process.

**CTRL-BC-002** — Documented RPO. Evidence: Business Continuity Plan. Result: **Ineffective**. No formal RPO.

**CTRL-CM-002** — SOC 2 Type II operating effectiveness. Evidence: not in pack. Result: **Evidence Not Provided**.

## Mapping

See `Control_Assessment.xlsx` and `Evidence_Mapping.xlsx`. Questionnaire IDs and risk IDs are carried forward into the traceability matrix.

## Independence note

Ratings are a TPRM design-and-evidence review, not a test of operating effectiveness over a period (which a SOC 2 Type II would better support).
