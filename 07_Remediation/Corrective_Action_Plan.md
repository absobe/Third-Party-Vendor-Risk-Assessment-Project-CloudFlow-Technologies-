# Corrective Action Plan

**This is a fictional cybersecurity case study created for educational and portfolio purposes.**

Nexus Financial Services will not treat Conditional Approval as a waiver of High residual risk. CloudFlow Technologies must complete or contractually commit to the actions below. Detailed fields live in `Remediation_Plan.xlsx`.

## CAP-001 / VR-001 — Subprocessor governance

**Action:** Establish documented risk-based security assessments for all critical subprocessors.  
**Success criteria:** Approved subprocessor assessment procedure and evidence for all critical subprocessors.  
**Owner:** Jordan Hale, CISO, CloudFlow Technologies. **Due:** 30 April 2026. **Priority:** High.

## CAP-002 / VR-002 — Backup testing

**Action:** Perform documented restoration testing at least quarterly for critical systems.  
**Success criteria:** Successful restoration test report with recovery timing.  
**Owner:** Taylor Nguyen. **Due:** 30 April 2026. **Priority:** High.

## CAP-003 / VR-003 — RPO

**Action:** Define and formally approve a platform-specific RPO.  
**Success criteria:** Documented RPO approved by technology and business owners (and committed to Nexus).  
**Owner:** Taylor Nguyen. **Due:** 31 March 2026. **Priority:** High.

## CAP-004 / VR-004 — Notification SLA

**Action:** 24-hour customer notice from confirmation of a material cybersecurity incident.  
**Success criteria:** Executed MSA/DPA security exhibit.  
**Owners:** Morgan Ellis; Samira Patel (Nexus Legal). **Due:** 31 March 2026. **Priority:** High.

## CAP-005 / VR-005 — SOC 2 Type II (accepted with conditions)

Provide Type II covering Security and Availability by 31 December 2026.

## CAP-006 / VR-006 — ISO 27001 (accepted with conditions)

Certification is not a go-live blocker. Maintain ISMS mapping and independent assurance.

## CAP-007 / VR-007 — Vulnerability window (accepted with conditions)

15-day target for High findings on internet-facing CRM assets by 30 June 2026.

## CAP-008 / VR-008 — Legacy MFA

MFA or compensating identity-aware proxy / retirement by 15 May 2026.

Production data load of Confidential Nexus records should not complete until CAP-003 and CAP-004 are contractually committed, and CAP-001, CAP-002, and CAP-008 have agreed plans with dates.
