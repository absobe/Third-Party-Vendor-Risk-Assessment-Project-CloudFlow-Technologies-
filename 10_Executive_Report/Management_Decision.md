# Management Decision

**This is a fictional cybersecurity case study created for educational and portfolio purposes.**

## Decision

**CONDITIONAL APPROVAL**

CloudFlow Technologies may be onboarded as the CloudFlow CRM provider for Nexus Financial Services only under documented security conditions. Overall residual risk at decision date: **High**. Overall vendor score: **74.4**. Assessment type: Initial Third-Party Vendor Security Assessment.

## Conditions (mandatory)

1. Remediation of high-risk findings VR-001, VR-002, VR-003, and VR-004 on or before the CAP due dates (31 March–30 April 2026), with VR-008 mitigated by 15 May 2026.  
2. Formal contractual security requirements in the MSA/DPA, including evidence rights.  
3. Annual reassessment (first due 6 March 2027).  
4. Evidence updates after PT, restore tests, policy review, and assurance reports.  
5. Incident notification within **24 hours** of confirmation of a material cybersecurity incident.  
6. Defined and approved recovery objectives (RTO already stated at 8 hours; **RPO must be documented**).  
7. Subprocessor review requirements: scored assessments for critical subprocessors and notice of material changes.

## Accepted with conditions (not go-live blockers)

- VR-005 SOC 2 Type I only — Type II by 31 December 2026.  
- VR-006 No ISO 27001 — accepted with continued independent assurance.  
- VR-007 30-day High vulnerability window — 15-day internet-facing High target by 30 June 2026.

## What was not decided

This document is not a commercial award, not a legal opinion, and not a certification of CloudFlow. It is a simulated security recommendation for portfolio education.
