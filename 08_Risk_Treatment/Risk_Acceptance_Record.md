# Risk Acceptance Record

**This is a fictional cybersecurity case study created for educational and portfolio purposes.**

The following risks are proposed for **acceptance with conditions** by Nexus Financial Services CISO. They are **not** High residual items. High residual risks VR-001–VR-004 are **not** accepted.

## VR-005 — SOC 2 Type I Only

| Field | Content |
| --- | --- |
| Residual | 2 × 3 = 6 Moderate |
| Why accept | Design-focused Type I plus policy evidence is enough for Conditional Approval of a non-payments CRM. |
| Condition | SOC 2 Type II (Security and Availability) by 31 December 2026, or equivalent independent examination. |
| Expiry / review | 31 December 2026 or next material incident, whichever first |
| Accepting officer (simulated) | Daniel Okonkwo, CISO |
